Quick Questions

How do interruptions work?

When something unexpected comes up, log the interruption inside ITZ-Time so you can adjust the rest of your schedule without losing track of what you planned.

What is the idea space for?

It’s a low-friction place to capture tasks or thoughts the moment they pop into your head. Whenever you’re ready to schedule them, turn any idea into a task with one tap.

Can I see tasks I’ve already completed?

Yes. ITZ-Time keeps a history of your past tasks so you can look back at how you actually spent your time and track your progress.

Privacy Policy

ITZ-Time is built to stay out of your way and respect your privacy. Your tasks, ideas, and schedule history are stored on your device (and your personal iCloud account if sync is enabled) and are never sold or shared with third-party advertisers.
