# ITZ-Time Privacy Policy

Last updated: Nov 22, 2026

## Summary

ITZ-Time does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything you create in the app stays on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud account. We have no servers and no way to see your data.

This policy explains how ITZ-Time ("the app"), developed by \[Developer Name\] ("we", "us"), handles information.

## Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. ITZ-Time does not require an account, and we never ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.

## Information You Create in the App

While you use ITZ-Time, you create content such as:

- Tasks and schedules
- Interruptions you log
- Ideas saved in the idea dump
- Your history of past tasks

This content is stored locally on your device. We cannot access, read or recover it.

## iCloud Sync

If iCloud is turned on for ITZ-Time, your content is synced between your devices through Apple's iCloud. It is stored in your own private iCloud account, which only you can access. We cannot see it.

Apple handles iCloud data under its own [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can turn off iCloud sync for ITZ-Time at any time in your device's Settings.

## Third-Party Services

ITZ-Time does not use any third-party services or SDKs. That means:

- No analytics or usage tracking
- No advertising
- No crash-reporting services
- No selling or sharing of data with anyone

The app does not track you across other apps or websites.

## Your Control Over Your Data

You are in full control of your data. You can edit or delete any task, interruption or idea inside the app. Deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. To remove synced data from iCloud, go to Settings > \[your name\] > iCloud > Manage Account Storage on your device.

## Data Security

Your data is protected by your device's built-in security and by Apple's iCloud encryption. Because we never receive your data, it cannot be exposed through our systems.

## Children's Privacy

ITZ-Time does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Because the app collects no personal data, it is safe for users of all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If we ever start collecting data, we will update this policy before doing so.
